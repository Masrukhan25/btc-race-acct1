# RegimeDip v1d — BTC race bot

Long-only cbBTC bot for the 24h BTC race: buys the whole cash balance into
oversold dips inside a healthy trend (RSI(14) under 45 with a 60-bar washout) or
into deep rebounds off a 720-bar fall (wick + reversal + 2:1 reward-to-risk),
each trade guarded by a stop, a take-profit or an armed trailing stop, at
most four entries per window.
